Based on the supplied 2015 CGP RGB values. Dark colours and neutral/semantic states are digital adaptations. Use Inter, or choose GEN-I under Font as its alias; this palette does not supply fonts or an official logo.

How can I keep the interface consistent?

Reuse the same typography, controls and spacing across each page. Let the content provide the visual hierarchy.

Use colour to indicate actions and state, and keep supporting information quiet.
