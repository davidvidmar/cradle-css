04

Numbers & dates

  • Amounts and other numbers are always right-aligned so units, tens and decimal places line up. Use tabular figures and the same number of decimal places within a column.
  • Dates are always centred. Use one consistent date format within a column.
  • Column headers follow the alignment of their values. Text stays left-aligned.
